ROUND 3 · RESEARCH

What the best apps do — and what we’re taking

I studied recent Apple Design Award winners (2023–2026) and the best-rated money apps. Round 3 borrows their principles, not their pixels: simple screens, one colour, and fun that lives in motion and type.

01 One answer per moment Seen in Flighty · Apple Design Award 2023

It aims to feel “boringly obvious”: each moment of a trip shows only what matters right now, set like airport signage.

→ Home shows the one number that matters now. Everything else is a tap away.

02 Huge numbers, one colour Seen in Revolut, Monzo, Cash App

Billboard-sized numerals on a calm neutral base. Colour arrives as one signature accent, never as decoration.

→ Every Round 3 direction is a neutral base plus exactly one accent colour.

03 Motion that confirms Seen in Copilot Money · top-rated money app of 2026

Smooth, native-feeling animation, and AI categories that learn from every correction you make.

→ When a number changes, it animates — and nothing else on screen moves.

04 Make the mundane tactile Seen in the (Not Boring) apps

A giant number up front, plus physics, haptics and sound, turns everyday utilities into things you want to touch.

→ Every entry gets one tactile moment: a roll, a weight change, a pen stroke.

05 Colour that means something Seen in Tide Guide · Apple Design Award 2026, Visuals

Animations that flow organically, with a palette that follows the real colour of the sky through the day.

→ Colour carries meaning — pace, money in vs out — and nothing else.

06 Words can be the delight Seen in grug · Apple Design Award 2026, Delight and Fun

A simple idea in a strong voice. The fun lives in the writing, not in mascots or graphics.

→ Microcopy with personality. The typography is the illustration.

BUILDABLE Every motion here maps to a native iOS API: SwiftUI numericText, TextRenderer (iOS 18+), variable fonts and Core Haptics.
