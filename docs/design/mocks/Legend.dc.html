ROUND 4 · WHAT YOU TOLD ME

Every section in a box. Every button looks like one.

N and O were easier to read because you could tell at a glance what each part of the screen was for. Round 4 makes that the rule. All five directions share the parts below, and each has its own layout, font and single accent colour (shown here in P’s indigo).

What made N and O easy to read
  1. 1Every section sits in its own container: a card, a tile, or rows separated by lines.
  2. 2The number that matters most is the biggest thing in its section.
  3. 3Every number has a label above it, so you never guess what it means.
  4. 4Colour marks data, not decoration. Green always means money in.
  5. 5Things you can tap look tappable: buttons are filled, and rows that open have a ›.
Each direction has three screens: Home, one sentence turned into 3 entries, and Edit by hand.
