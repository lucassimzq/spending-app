# Spending app

**Kira** is a spending tracker for iPhone. You say it, type it or screenshot it, and it becomes entries, with money in and money out up front and categories one tap away. Kira is a working name (Malay *kira*, "to count").

- [`ios/`](ios/README.md) is the iPhone app: SwiftUI, iOS 26 and later, iPhone only for now.
- [`docs/design/`](docs/design/README.md) is the design: six rounds of mocks, screenshots of every screen, and notes on how the AI works. The app follows Round 6.

The [iOS workflow](.github/workflows/ios.yml) runs the tests and builds the app on GitHub's Macs whenever `ios/` changes.
